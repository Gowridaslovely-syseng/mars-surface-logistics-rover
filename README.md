# 🚀 Mars Surface Logistics Rover

> A model-based systems engineering project exploring the conceptual design of an autonomous rover for cargo transportation on the Martian surface.

---

## Overview

This project was developed as part of graduate coursework in Systems Engineering at Stevens Institute of Technology.

The objective was to design an autonomous logistics rover capable of transporting cargo between a Mars lander and a surface habitat while operating under challenging environmental conditions including communication delays, rough terrain, dust exposure, and limited maintenance opportunities.
